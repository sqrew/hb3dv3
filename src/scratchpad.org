* Random
   Visual Polish:
  - Pulsing effects: Stars could pulsate brightness, black holes could have event horizon effects
  - Particle trails: Comets with tails, black holes with accretion disk particles

  Physics Enhancements:
  - Tidal forces: Objects get stretched/torn apart when too close to massive bodies
  - Atmospheric drag: Gas giants slow down objects passing through their "atmosphere"

  Gameplay Features:
  - Resource drops: Destroyed asteroids drop collectible materials
  - Destructible bodies: Asteroids break into smaller chunks when hit enough times
  - Environmental storytelling: Each body type could spawn with randomized "lore" (ancient ruins on planets, etc.)







  Orbital Mechanics Weaponry

  Slingshot Bullets: Bullets that gain speed when passing close to large bodies
  Orbital Mines: Weapons that enter stable orbit around planets/stars
  Gravity Wells: Deployable devices that create temporary gravitational fields
  Implementation: Extend bullet physics with orbital velocity calculations


  Tidal Forces & Destruction
  Roche Limit: Objects get torn apart when too close to massive bodies
  Tidal Heating: Objects glow and take damage near massive bodies
  Implementation: Add tidal force calculation to compute shaders, progressive destruction particles


  Gravitational Wave Generation

  Binary Systems: Two massive bodies orbiting create ripple effects
  Wave Propagation: Distortions spread outward affecting other objects
  Implementation: Generate periodic force waves from accelerating masses






    Visually Spectacular:
  - Gravitational Lensing - Projectiles and light bend around massive objects, creating curved bullet trajectories near black holes
  - Accretion Disks - Swirling matter spirals into black holes/neutron stars with realistic orbital decay
  - Tidal Forces - Large objects get visibly stretched/deformed when approaching massive bodies
  - Shock Waves - Expanding spherical force fields from explosions that push entities outward

  Gameplay-Enhancing:
  - Magnetic Fields - Charged projectiles follow curved helical paths around magnetized bodies
  - Solar Wind - Constant outward force from stars that affects small objects and creates "gravity wells" you can surf
  - Roche Limit Effects - Large asteroids break apart into debris fields when they get too close to planets
  - Lagrange Points - Gravitational equilibrium zones where you can park safely between massive bodies

  Emergent Complexity:
  - Gravitational Waves - Massive accelerating objects create expanding ripples that temporarily alter gravity fields
  - Dark Matter Halos - Invisible mass clouds that only affect gravity, creating mysterious force regions
  - Frame Dragging - Rotating black holes drag spacetime, creating spiral gravity effects
