  - Collision detection with elastic response including angular momentum transfer (lines 179-257)

  - Lifecycle management with age/lifetime tracking

  4. Good Integration Patterns
  - Large bodies properly register with physics system
  - Bidirectional sync between large_body entities and physics GPU data
  - Event system for solar winds and death sequences

  ---
  The Bad/Concerning:


  1. Duplicate Code in physics.rs
  2. update_gravity() (lines 730-919) and update_gravity_batch() (lines 496-727) are nearly identical
  3. Both have the same double-readback synchronization issue
  4. Should be refactored into shared implementation

  5. Index Management Fragility (large_body.rs:805-816)
  6. When removing a body from physics, manually adjusting all higher indices
  7. Error-prone and doesn't scale well
  8. Consider using a stable handle/ID system instead of raw indices

  9. Padding Confusion (physics.rs:21-36, physics.wgsl:4-17)
  10. 64-byte alignment with manual padding is error-prone
  11. Total size: 34 + 4 + 34 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 52 bytes not 64
  12. Are you actually hitting alignment issues? bytemuck::Pod would catch this.

  13. No Timestep Validation
  14. delta_time passed directly to GPU without clamping
  15. Large timesteps (lag spike) could cause physics explosions
  16. Consider max timestep capping (e.g., 33ms)

  17. Softening Length Too Small (physics.wgsl:39)
  const MIN_DISTANCE_SQUARED: f32 = 0.01; // distance = 0.1 units
  For bodies with radius 1.0-80.0, this is way too small for numerical stability.

  10. CPU Fallback Incomplete (physics.rs:921-981)
  - CPU fallback doesn't implement:
    - Frame-dragging effects
    - Collision detection
    - Explosion forces
    - N-body simulation
  - Will behave very differently if GPU unavailable

  3. Medium: Add delta_time clamping

Physically Realistic Features

  1. Roche Limit & Tidal Disruption
  - Bodies that venture too close to massive objects get torn apart by tidal forces
  - Could spawn a debris field of smaller bodies when disruption occurs
  - Visually dramatic and physically accurate
  - Implementation: Check distance vs. (primary_mass / satellite_mass)^(1/3) * satellite_radius * 2.46 and break up body into fragments

  2. Accretion Disk Formation
  - Objects spiraling into black holes/stars form visible particle disks
  - Could emit radiation/light effects as matter gets compressed
  - Implementation: Spawn high-velocity particles in orbital paths when bodies get very close but don't collide

  3. Gravitational Slingshot Boost Indicator
  - Show trajectory prediction lines when approaching bodies at high speed
  - Visual/audio feedback when player executes a successful gravity assist
  - Award bonus multiplier for stylish maneuvers
  - Implementation: Calculate velocity change before/after close approach, spawn particle trail effect

  4. Tidal Locking
  - Bodies in close orbit gradually synchronize their rotation to always face each other
  - Adds realism to long-running binary systems
  - Implementation: Torque calculation based on tidal bulge asymmetry, gradually adjust angular_velocity toward orbital period

  Visual/Aesthetic Additions

  5. Gravitational Lensing
  - Light bending near massive objects (especially black holes)
  - Screen-space shader effect that warps the skybox/background
  - Implementation: Post-process shader that samples from distorted UV coordinates based on body mass & distance

  6. Particle Ring Systems
  - Saturn-like rings around gas giants and planets
  - Could be debris from destroyed moons
  - Implementation: Spawn many small particles in stable circular orbits with thin vertical distribution

  7. Gravitational Wave Shockwaves
  - When large bodies collide or orbit rapidly, emit visible ripples in space
  - Could apply screen shake or distortion effects
  - Implementation: Expanding ring effect similar to explosions but with oscillating intensity

  8. Comet Tails
  - Small bodies with elliptical orbits develop particle tails when near stars
  - Tail always points away from nearest star
  - Implementation: Spawn particles with velocity based on solar wind direction, longer lifetime

  Gameplay-Oriented Features

  9. Lagrange Point Markers
  - Show stable L4/L5 points in binary systems
  - Could spawn collectibles or enemies there
  - Strategic positions for player to camp/ambush
  - Implementation: Calculate L4/L5 positions ±60° along orbit, spawn marker primitives

  10. Body Merging System
  - Colliding bodies can merge into larger bodies (mass/momentum conservation)
  - BlackHole + Star → Larger BlackHole (dramatic!)
  - Could trigger special effects based on merger type
  - Implementation: In collision code, create new body with combined mass/momentum, destroy originals

  11. Capture Mechanics
  - Small objects can be "captured" into orbit when conditions are right
  - Player could collect mini-moons that orbit and provide bonuses
  - Enemies could orbit you and need to be shaken off
  - Implementation: Check relative velocity vs escape velocity, apply gentle binding force

  12. Gravity Wells as Tactical Space
  - Hide behind planets to break line-of-sight
  - Use gravity to bend bullets around obstacles
  - Enemies get "stuck" in deep gravity wells
  - Implementation: Already have the physics, just need AI to react tactically

  Complex Dynamic Systems

  13. Stellar Evolution Chain
  - Stars age over time: Star → Red Giant → Supernova → Neutron Star/Black Hole
  - Size/mass/color changes as they evolve
  - Implementation: Age-based state machine with smooth transitions, emit particles during supernova

  14. Orbital Resonances
  - Bodies in 2:1, 3:2 ratio orbits create beautiful stable patterns
  - Could be puzzle elements ("create a stable 3-body resonance")
  - Implementation: Monitor orbital periods, spawn visual markers when resonance detected

  15. Asteroid Belt Generator
  - Spawn a field of small asteroids in a ring
  - Could be mining resource zones
  - Collisions create fragments
  - Implementation: Spawn many small LauncherMass bodies with slightly varied orbital parameters

  16. Multi-Star Systems
  - Binary, trinary, and figure-8 orbit systems
  - You already have binary pair spawning - extend to more complex configurations
  - Implementation: Pre-calculate stable configurations (like figure-8 choreography) and spawn with precise initial conditions

  Exotic/Sci-Fi Additions

  17. Wormhole Pairs
  - Two bodies that teleport objects passing through them
  - Could create strategic shortcuts or puzzle elements
  - Implementation: New LargeBodyType::Wormhole, check collisions and teleport position/velocity to paired wormhole

  18. Micro Black Holes
  - Player-spawned temporary black holes as weapons
  - Small radius but intense gravity
  - Implementation: New weapon type that spawns short-lived BlackHole with high mass, small radius

  19. Gravitational Mines
  - Deployable objects that pull enemies in or create chaotic orbital patterns
  - Implementation: Similar to large bodies but with trigger radius and special behaviors

  20. Plasma Ejections
  - Stars periodically eject coronal mass ejections that push objects
  - Could be hazards or boost opportunities
  - Implementation: Directional explosion events spawned from star position
